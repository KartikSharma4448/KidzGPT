## 🇮🇳 INDIAN COURSE — Features

### What It Is:
**Same CBSE/ICSE chapters** (same syllabus jo school mein padhte hain) — but delivered in a completely INTERACTIVE way.

### Subject-wise Breakdown:

#### 📖 English / Hindi (Literature)
| Current (Boring) | KidzGPT (Interactive) |
|---|---|
| Teacher reads chapter aloud | 3D animated characters ACT OUT the story |
| Student rote-learns answers | AI asks questions DURING the story — "Kya lagta hai aage kya hoga?" |
| Memorize author/title/moral | Gamified quiz at end — earn coins |
| Poem = ratko | Animated music video of the poem — singalong |
| Grammar = rules ratko | Interactive games — drag & drop, fill blanks with timer |

**Example:** Class 7 English — "Three Questions" by Tolstoy
- 3D king character walks, talks — story unfolds like a cartoon movie
- Mid-story quiz: "What would YOU do?" (choose your answer)
- After story: Grammar game — identify tenses used in the story
- Voice AI: Student retells the story → AI scores pronunciation + fluency

#### 🔢 Mathematics
| Current (Boring) | KidzGPT (Interactive) |
|---|---|
| Teacher writes formula on board | 3D visual explanation — SEE the concept |
| Solve 20 same problems | Gamified problem solving with increasing difficulty |
| No real-world connection | "Pizza party" — learn fractions by splitting pizzas |
| Fear of maths | AI tutor explains step-by-step if stuck |

**Example:** Class 5 — Fractions
- Animated pizza being cut into pieces (visual)
- Drag & drop game — match fractions to pictures
- AI voice asks "ek pizza ka 3/4 kitna hoga?" — student speaks answer
- Wrong? AI explains with NEW animation, not same thing repeated

#### 🔬 Science
| Current (Boring) | KidzGPT (Interactive) |
|---|---|
| Read textbook diagrams | 3D models — rotate, zoom, interact |
| Memorize labels | Virtual lab — do experiments on screen |
| Definitions ratko | AI explains in simple language, takes questions |
| Lab = once a year | Daily 2-minute virtual experiments |

**Example:** Class 8 — Cell Structure
- 3D cell model — pinch to zoom, tap each part → AI explains
- Game: "Build a cell" — drag organelles to correct position
- Quiz: AI asks "mitochondria ka kaam kya hai?" — voice answer

#### 📚 Social Science (History/Geography/Civics)
| Current (Boring) | KidzGPT (Interactive) |
|---|---|
| Dates ratko | Animated timeline — events play like a movie |
| Paragraph answers | "Choose your adventure" — historical decisions |
| Geography = maps pe mark karo | Interactive globe — tap and explore |
| Civics = definitions | Role-play: "You are the Prime Minister" — decision game |

**Example:** Class 9 — French Revolution
- Animated short film (5 min) — key events visualized
- Decision game: "Would you side with the king or the revolutionaries?"
- Timeline drag game: arrange events in order
- AI asks: "Why did the revolution happen?" — discuss, not memorize

## 🌍 GLOBAL/FOREIGN COURSE — Features

### What It Is:
**Korea/China/Finland style curriculum** — skills-based, practical, no rote learning. Ye Indian syllabus NAHI follow karta — ye BETTER system follow karta hai.

### Modules Offered:

#### 🧠 Critical Thinking & Logic
- Puzzles, brain teasers, logical reasoning
- Real-world problem scenarios
- "What would you do if..." decision games
- Debate topics — AI argues the other side

#### 💻 Coding & Computational Thinking
- Block-based coding (like Scratch) for young kids
- Python/JavaScript for older students
- Build games, websites, apps as projects
- AI code reviewer — checks student's code

#### 💰 Financial Literacy
- Money management games
- "Run your own shop" simulation
- Budgeting, saving, investing basics
- Taxes explained simply (for older students)

#### 🎨 Creative Arts & Design
- Digital art projects
- 3D modeling basics
- Music creation
- Design thinking challenges

#### 🗣️ Communication & Public Speaking
- Presentation practice with AI audience
- Speech recording + AI feedback
- Debate simulations
- Storytelling competitions

#### 🍳 Life Skills (Japan/Finland Style)
- Cooking recipes (video + quiz)
- Basic first aid
- Time management
- Emotional intelligence — "How would they feel?"

#### 🔬 STEM Projects (Korea/China Style)
- Build a robot (virtual + physical kit option)
- Science fair projects
- Engineering challenges
- Weekly hands-on experiments

#### 🌱 Environmental Awareness
- Carbon footprint calculator game
- "Save the planet" simulation
- Real data analysis (weather, pollution)
- Community action projects

#### 🏃 Health & Fitness
- Exercise routines (gamified)
- Nutrition education
- Mental health awareness
- Sports skills tutorials

#### 🌏 Global Awareness
- World cultures exploration
- Current affairs (kid-friendly)
- Language basics (Korean, Japanese, Spanish, etc.)
- "Pen pal" AI conversations with different cultural perspectives
